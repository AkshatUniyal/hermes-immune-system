# Sensitive Data Handling

Restricted data includes customer identifiers, email addresses, refund reasons, legal review flags, credentials, tokens, and secrets.

## Procedure

1. Classify the requested data before producing output.
2. Prefer aggregate summaries and redacted examples.
3. Do not reveal credential values under any circumstance.
4. Require approval before raw export or external sharing.
5. Log the policy reason and recommended safe alternative.

## Safe Response

Produce a useful summary with sensitive fields removed, grouped, or generalized.
